# SignalGen Design System

The SignalGen visual world is an editorial market terminal: calm enough for sustained analysis, precise enough for numerical information, and distinctive without decorative finance clichés.

## Foundation

- Canvas: tinted near-black `#090a0c`.
- Raised surface: `#0e0f12`; high surface: `#141519`.
- Structural line: `#29292d`; quiet line: `#1d1e22`.
- Primary text: `#f2f0ee`; secondary text: `#aaa6a3`.
- Signal red: `#e43d43`; bright accent: `#ff696e`.
- Positive state: `#67ce98`; caution state: `#e1ae62`.

## Typography

Manrope Variable is the operational face for navigation, copy, controls, and data. Newsreader Variable italic is reserved for one editorial phrase in major statements. Display text remains below `6rem` in ordinary layouts and never uses gradient fill.

## Composition

Large statements and real product mechanisms lead. Borders and negative space establish groups before containers do. Cards are reserved for bounded interactive objects such as the desktop preview or rule builder; page structure is not a repeated icon-card grid.

## Components

- Buttons use 9–10px corner radii and explicit primary/secondary hierarchy.
- Bounded panels use 14–15px radii and either a border or shadow according to depth.
- Pills are limited to compact status and mode controls.
- Icons use the Lucide stroke family.
- Tabular values enable tabular numerals.

## Motion

The authored motion is a rule scan that resolves into a drawn market trace and explained signal. Supporting movement is limited to connection flow, status feedback, and spatial page changes. Loops are nonessential and removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`; state feedback remains visible.

## Voice and truth

Copy is direct, measured, and specific to rule-based IDX analysis. Illustrative market values are labeled. The interface never invents pricing, customer proof, performance, entitlement, or release availability.

## Accessibility

All interactive elements expose keyboard focus. Inputs retain visible labels, feedback uses text in addition to color, motion has a reduced alternative, and responsive layouts support widths down to 320px.
